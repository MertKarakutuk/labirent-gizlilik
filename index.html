Privacy Policy

Minotaur's Riddle: Maze Quiz (Turkish name: Labirentten Kaçış; the "App")
Developer: BlackWood Games ("we", "us")
Last updated: 2026-11-19

Summary

The App needs no account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your game progress stays on your device. The App shows ads (Google AdMob) and, only if you accept, records anonymous gameplay events (Google Firebase Analytics) to improve the game.

Who is responsible

BlackWood Games is the developer of the App and is responsible for the data described here. Privacy contact: blackwoodgame.dev@gmail.com.

What is stored only on your device

Game progress (countries, stages, stars, hints, calendar, settings, language) and your consent choice are saved locally on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob. Ads are shown only after a successfully completed stage or when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent screen is shown first and ads use your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings and, in regions where the consent screen is required, change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Analytics

The App records a small fixed set of gameplay events: stage started, won, failed or quit; country unlocked; first stage finished; ad shown; rewarded ad completed; question reported; language changed. Each event carries only numbers and short codes (such as country code, stage number, stars, error count, question ID, language code, ad type). Events never contain your name, email address, phone number or free text, and we do not set a user ID.

Analytics are off by default and are switched on only if you accept. The first time you open the App, it asks you in every region; Accept and Decline are equally easy, and the game works the same either way. If you decline, no analytics event is sent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings (Anonymous gameplay statistics). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's own consent form is shown instead of our screen, and analytics stay switched off there. Firebase assigns a pseudonymous app instance ID and may collect device and operating-system information and approximate location. These events are not linked to your name, email address or phone number. We use the shortest event-data retention period offered in Firebase settings.

Question reports

If you report a question, the App stores the report on your device and sends an analytics event containing only the question ID (see Analytics). The App does not send any text you type.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or precise location, and we do not use accounts or sign-in.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete what we can identify.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the App. Data held by Google (AdMob, Firebase) is kept for the periods set by Google and our Firebase settings. Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which data belongs to you; to make a request (access, deletion, objection) email us at the address in the Contact section, tell us your approximate install date and, if you can, your advertising ID, and we will delete or send what we can identify. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings, which breaks the link with earlier data. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or Turkey) you may have additional rights under local data-protection law, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

International processing

Google may process data in countries other than your own. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. If our data practices change, we will update this page before releasing the change.
